Which path fits you?

Five quick questions. Pick the answer that sounds most like you, and we will point you to IT, computer science or information systems.

1. What sounds most fun to you?
2. Which project would you pick?
3. How do you feel about numbers and math?
4. Which question would you rather chase?
5. Where do you see yourself in five years?

Your result appears once three of your answers point the same way, or when you have answered all five.
